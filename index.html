How to play

  • Players take turns placing X or O on a 3×3 grid.
  • Get three of your marks in a row, column or diagonal to win.
  • If all nine squares fill with no line, the round is a draw.
  • The player who goes first alternates each round.

Computer levels

  • Easy: picks squares at random. Good for beginners.
  • Medium: plays well, but slips up now and then.
  • Unbeatable: never misses. Your best result is a draw.

Questions

Who should go first?

X always moves first. Taking the center or a corner on your first move gives the best chances.

Can the second player win?

Yes, if the first player makes a mistake. With perfect play from both sides, every game ends in a draw.

Do scores save?

Scores last until you close or reload the page, or press Reset scores.
